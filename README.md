## 🔬 Research & Thesis Interests

I’m interested in research at the intersection of **core AI, automated planning, and reliable intelligent systems**. Potential thesis directions include using **PDDL and constraint-based methods** to solve complex planning problems, or designing **fault-tolerant AI systems** that can detect and recover from failures while remaining understandable.

### Areas I’m Exploring

1. **Automated Planning** — PDDL, plan generation, and search
2. **Constraint Satisfaction** — CSP modeling, constraint solving, and optimization
3. **Knowledge Representation & Reasoning** — representing facts, rules, and goals for AI
4. **Explainable AI** — making AI decisions easier to understand
5. **Machine Learning** — learning patterns from data
6. **Deep Learning** — neural networks for complex tasks
7. **Intelligent Agents** — systems that perceive, plan, and act toward goals

These are areas I’m exploring as possible research directions for a future thesis or project.
